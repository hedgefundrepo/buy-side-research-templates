Item 1. Business

ExampleCo designs and sells molded plastic housewares to retailers. This text is fictional and exists only to test the risk-factor diff script.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our business faces many risks. The risks described below are not the only ones we face.

Risks Related to Our Business

We depend on a small number of suppliers for key inputs

We buy most of our resin from three suppliers. A disruption at any of them could delay production and increase our costs.

Input cost increases could reduce our margins

Increases in the cost of resin and freight could reduce our gross margin if we are unable to pass them on to customers through higher prices.

Our debt agreements contain covenants that restrict our operations

Our credit agreement requires us to maintain a maximum leverage ratio. A breach could allow lenders to accelerate repayment of our borrowings.

We rely on information systems to run our business

We rely on information systems to process orders, manage inventory and report results. A cybersecurity incident could disrupt operations.

Our largest customer accounts for a significant share of our revenue

Our largest retail customer accounted for a significant share of net sales. The loss of this customer, or a reduction in its orders, could materially reduce our revenue.

General Risk Factors

Changes in tax law could affect our results

Changes in tax rates or tax rules in the jurisdictions where we operate could increase our effective tax rate.
